# Source Policy

Last reviewed: 2026-08-18

## Principle

Venue quality is an admission signal, not a substitute for reading. Use the source tiers to control noise, then assess every work on its own problem, argument, evidence, novelty, and limitations. The registry is a maintained starting point rather than an exhaustive canon.

## Chinese Scholarship

Use the current CSSCI source-journal cycle as the default gate for Chinese academic journals. The official Nanjing University evaluation center records that the CSSCI 2025-2026 source, expanded-source, and collected-journal lists were approved in August 2025:

- https://cssrac.nju.edu.cn/gywm/zxjj/index.html
- https://cssrac.nju.edu.cn/gywm/lxbz/20230718/i249257.html

The machine registry includes a conservative seed set of established core law journals. Verify current status and the actual article page; do not infer that every article in a listed journal is excellent. Also search high-quality interdisciplinary CSSCI journals when the legal contribution is central.

Chinese discovery may require authenticated or browser-based access to CNKI, the journal's official site or official WeChat account, National Social Sciences Database, university repositories, and publisher pages. Search snippets alone are insufficient.

## English and International Scholarship

Admit work from one of these routes:

1. Leading general law reviews identified by current Washington and Lee rankings and independently strong scholarly reputation.
2. Established peer-reviewed law journals and specialist journals in technology law, AI and law, privacy, empirical legal studies, law and society, regulation, and adjacent fields.
3. A strong interdisciplinary journal or selective conference when law or governance is a central contribution, not an afterthought.

Use Washington and Lee only as a venue-screening aid; never import its citation metrics into paper ranking:

- https://managementtools4.wlu.edu/LawJournals/Default.aspx
- https://managementtools4.wlu.edu/LawJournals/Default2.aspx

For journals outside the seed registry, verify editorial ownership, peer-review or editorial process, publisher identity, indexing, continuity, and fit. Reject predatory or pay-to-publish venues with opaque governance.

## Working Papers and Preprints

Working papers are essential for frontier detection but receive no automatic prestige. SSRN's Legal Scholarship Network is a discovery channel, not a quality certification:

- https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/EN/lsn/

Admit a working paper when at least one strong provenance signal exists: a recognized university research-paper series; an established research institute; disclosed forthcoming placement in a qualifying journal; a selective conference; or unusually transparent and rigorous work whose contribution can be independently assessed. Label it `working-paper` or `preprint`.

## Reports and Policy Papers

Admit research reports from accountable public bodies and established research institutions when they contain original analysis, evidence, or a serious legal framework. Examples include courts and law reform bodies, NIST, OECD, Council of Europe, European Union institutions, UN bodies, Stanford HAI, Ada Lovelace Institute, and comparable Chinese public or academic institutions. Label these as reports; do not present them as peer-reviewed journal articles.

## Discovery Infrastructure

- OpenAlex: broad discovery and open-access metadata — https://help.openalex.org/api/
- Crossref: DOI, date, venue, and publisher metadata — https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/tips-for-using-the-crossref-rest-api/
- DOI resolver: canonical work link — https://doi.org/

Crossref and OpenAlex metadata can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Resolve conflicts using the publisher or issuing institution. Never rank by OpenAlex citation count or Crossref `is-referenced-by-count`.

## Access Labels

- `full_text`: the analyst inspected the complete paper or report.
- `abstract_only`: only the abstract or executive summary was inspected.

Paywalls do not automatically disqualify a work. If no lawful full text is found, retain it only when its abstract supports a useful, carefully bounded evaluation and use the mandatory abstract-only warning.
